Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Tondlift: Gym Workout Tracker (“Tondlift” or “the app”) handles your information. It applies to the Android app (package com.tondlift.gym.workout.tracker) and the iOS app.

Tondlift is a workout log for strength training. You can track sets, reps, weights and RIR, see 1RM estimates, records and charts, build routines, and add photos and notes to exercises. The app works offline.

The app is developed by Botond Sulyok, an individual developer based in Hungary (“I”, “me”).

Information we collect

I do not collect any personal data.

  • There is no user account or registration.
  • I don’t have my own server or backend. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to me.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs other than Google Play Billing (Android) and Apple StoreKit (iOS), which are used only for the subscription.

The app does not handle body weight, body measurements, or any data from Health Connect or Apple HealthKit. It only handles training data you enter yourself, such as the weight lifted and the number of reps, and it keeps that data on your device or in your own cloud backup.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device:

  • Workout data – exercises, routines, workouts, sets, weights, reps, RIR and notes – in a local SQLite database.
  • Settings – such as theme, kg/lb units and language – in local key-value storage.
  • Photos you add – in the app’s private storage.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this from your device. Cloud backups in your own Google or iCloud account are separate (see Backups).

Photos

You can choose to add photos to your exercises. You can take a photo with your camera app or pick one from your gallery using the system photo picker.

  • The app does not ask for camera or storage permission.
  • It can only access the image you select.
  • Photos stay on your device. They may also be included in your own backups, as described below.

Backups

Automatic cloud backup (to your own account)

Your data can be backed up automatically to your own cloud account. I have no access to these backups.

  • Android: the app uses Android’s built-in Auto Backup. If backup is turned on for your phone, the database and your settings are saved to your Google Account (Google Drive), encrypted. Photos are not included in this cloud backup; they are only included in a direct device-to-device transfer. You can turn backup off in your phone’s system settings.
  • iOS: if you are signed in to iCloud, the app saves your data, including photos, to your private iCloud database (CloudKit private database). Only your Apple ID can access it; I can’t see it. You can turn this off in your iCloud settings.

These backups are handled by Google and Apple under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy.

Manual export and import

When you ask for it, the app can create a ZIP backup (data and photos) or a CSV spreadsheet. The file is saved where you choose (system file picker) or shared with the app you choose (share menu). After that, the file is under your control.

The app can also restore your data from an earlier ZIP backup.

Purchases and subscriptions

Tondlift requires a Tondlift Premium subscription, billed monthly or yearly. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

  • Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple StoreKit / the App Store (iOS).
  • I never receive your payment or card details.
  • The app only checks whether your subscription is active, and caches this status locally on your device.

Google and Apple process your purchase information under their own privacy policies (linked above).

Data sharing

I do not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone.

The only external parties involved are Google and Apple, through the backup and purchase features described above.

Data retention and deletion

  • On my side: I don’t store any of your data, so there is nothing to keep or delete.
  • On your device: your data is kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud backups: you can delete them in your Google Account (Android) or in your iCloud settings (iOS).
  • Exported files: ZIP and CSV files you export are under your control, and you can delete them at any time.

Children’s privacy

Tondlift is not intended for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights (GDPR)

The data controller is Botond Sulyok (Hungary), contact: botond.sulyok99@gmail.com.

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability.

Because I don’t process any data on a server, I don’t hold any of your data, so there is nothing for me to hand over or delete. You can exercise these rights directly on your device:

  • view and edit your data in the app,
  • delete your data in the app, or uninstall the app,
  • export your data as a ZIP backup or CSV file,
  • delete your cloud backups in your Google Account or iCloud settings.

If you have any questions, email me. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes to this policy
